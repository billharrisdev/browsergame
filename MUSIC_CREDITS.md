## Verification and selection

Catalog metadata and direct MP3 filenames were checked on October 11, 2026:
https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/pieces.json

The official track-page template provides the CC BY 4.0 attribution above.
The composer's FAQ explicitly describes attribution in video games:
https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/faq.html

This is a mix of familiar internet music and gameplay-suited electronic/chiptune
tracks, not a ranked list of twelve equally popular songs. Examples of independent
coverage of the recognizable selections:

- Monkeys Spinning Monkeys: https://www.npr.org/2024/09/24/g-s1-23951/monkeys-spinning-youtube-tiktok-viral-social-media-music
- Fluffing a Duck: https://www.vulture.com/2024/01/fluffing-a-duck-kevin-mcleod-interview.html

The song picker contains previews; the Music credits dialog provides title,
artist, source link, ISRC, and license information for every bundled track.
